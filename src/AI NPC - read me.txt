AI NPC - setting up the model that answers you
==============================================

The mod needs somewhere for the characters' replies to come from. The installer
asked who you are: someone here to play, or someone writing an add on for AI NPC.
This file repeats what each answer needs, because an installer screen cannot be
reopened and the links on it cannot be clicked.

Whatever you chose, you can change it later in game: open the Cyber Engine Tweaks
overlay, tab AI NPC, tab Setup. Nothing here is permanent.

If you are here to play: OPENROUTER is the answer, and the section below is all you
need. The Claude section after it is a developer lane, not a second way to play.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPENROUTER - the supported way to play
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

One account reaches models from every vendor, and you pay only for what you use. On a
good model that is about a dollar a month of normal play. This is the lane the mod is
built around and the only one supported for playing.

  1. Make an account at   https://openrouter.ai
  2. Create a key at      https://openrouter.ai/keys
  3. Start the game, open the CET overlay, tab AI NPC, tab Setup,
     paste the key and press Test.

This lane needs nothing else installed: ai_npc.dll talks to OpenRouter itself.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLAUDE SUBSCRIPTION - for mod developers, not for playing
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This lane exists so that people writing characters and add ons for AI NPC can test
their work against a real model without paying per message. It is not a second way to
play the mod, and it is not supported as one.

Playing through a coding agent subscription is outside what these plans are sold for.
Anthropic states that the limits on Claude Pro and Max assume ordinary individual use
of Claude Code, and it may limit or suspend an account without warning first.

There is no Codex option in this installer, and that is not an oversight. OpenAI's
terms of use forbid extracting Output automatically or programmatically, with no
exception written next to it, so a mod reading what Codex prints cannot be offered
here whatever it is used for.

Read the terms and decide for yourself:

     https://www.anthropic.com/legal/consumer-terms
     https://code.claude.com/docs/en/legal-and-compliance

If you are playing rather than building, use OpenRouter. It costs about a dollar a
month and nothing about it is at risk.

The rest of this section is for the developers it is meant for. It runs Claude on the
machine through your own Claude subscription, so nothing is billed per message. It
needs a paid plan: Claude Pro or Claude Max. A free Claude account will not work.

  1. Install Claude for Windows from    https://claude.com/download
     Open it and sign in with the account that carries your subscription.

  2. Start the game, open the CET overlay, tab AI NPC, tab Setup. It reports whether
     Claude was found and whether you are signed in, and Test sends one real message.

If the window says Claude was not found, it is installed somewhere the game cannot see.
Paste the full path to claude.exe in the Command field of that same tab. It is usually:

     %USERPROFILE%\.local\bin\claude.exe

If Claude for Windows does not give you the "claude" command at all, install it this way
instead: press the Windows key, type PowerShell, open it, paste this line and press
Enter:

     irm https://claude.ai/install.ps1 | iex

Then type "claude" and choose "Sign in with Claude".

What answers you is always the account you signed in with. If an Anthropic API key
happens to be set on your machine, the mod removes it before calling Claude, so it
cannot be billed to you by mistake.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHEN SOMETHING DOES NOT WORK
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The phone says [NO SIGNAL] with a reason attached. The CET overlay, tab Setup, says
more: which provider is selected, what it is pointed at, and what is missing. Press
Test there to send one real message and read the answer.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THIS IS NOT A FORK
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AI NPC was written from scratch. It is not a fork of Generative Texting, of
Immersive Companion Generative Texting, or of any other mod in that family, and it
carries no line of their code and none of their prompts. It is not one more
variant - it is a different mod that came from the same idea.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUT IT WOULD NOT EXIST WITHOUT THEM
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I played GENERATIVE TEXTING and IMMERSIVE COMPANION GENERATIVE TEXTING long before
I wrote a line of this. That is where the idea came from, and I would never have
thought of building it if I had not seen theirs working first.

Thank you to their authors for clearing the road: the notion that a character
could answer on the in-game phone, the approach of feeding quest state into what
they say, and jackie_dead - a contact who answers from the far side of their own
death, which is still one of the best ideas in this corner of Cyberpunk modding.

Some things are kept on purpose so that anyone arriving from those mods finds what
they already know: T still opens the chat from the phone, and the characters carry
the same contact names.

Built on RED4ext, redscript, Codeware, RedData, RedFileSystem and
Mod Settings. Thank you to WopsS, jac3km4, psiberx and rayshader. Cyber Engine
Tweaks is optional and unlocks the Setup and Journal windows.

The mod's own code is MIT licensed. The replies are generated by a model you choose
and pay for; your messages go to that provider and nowhere else. No key ships with
the mod.

AI NPC is an unofficial fan modification. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or
sponsored by CD Projekt Red. Cyberpunk 2077 and everything in it are theirs.
